What do you want to manage?

  • Dishes — the menu: category, price, spice level, vegetarian, available today.
  • Reservations — table bookings: date, time, guests and status.
  • Orders — takeaway and delivery orders and their status.
  • Set menus — price, lunch or dinner, weekdays only, active.
  • Drinks — the drinks on the menu and their prices.

The data lives in the server's memory: anything created here goes back to the starting data when the server restarts.
