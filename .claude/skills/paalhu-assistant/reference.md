## About us

- Family-run by the Nahasapeemapetilon family since 2011. The father cooks, the mother welcomes the guests, and the team has nine people.
- The parents are from Kolkata, West Bengal, and came to Madrid in 2004.
- What makes PAALHU different: homemade spice blends, naan baked to order, mild-to-hot spice levels, vegetarian and vegan dishes, takeaway and home delivery.
